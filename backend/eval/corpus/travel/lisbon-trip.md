# Lisbon trip

A long weekend in Lisbon in April.

## Train

The train to Lisbon departs at 9:40 from platform 4 at Porto Campanha station. The journey takes
about three hours. Seats are reserved in coach 7.

## Hotel

The hotel is Hotel Alfama. Check-in is from 3 pm and the room is booked for three nights.
Breakfast is not included.

## Sightseeing

Belem Tower is open from 10:00 to 18:00 and is closed on Mondays. Buy tickets online to avoid
the queue.

## Budget

The whole trip budget is 450 euros, not counting the train tickets.
