Rice cooking notes

White rice: use twice its volume of water. Bring to the boil, cover, and simmer on the lowest
heat for 18 minutes. Then leave it covered for 10 minutes without lifting the lid.

Brown rice: use two and a half times its volume of water and simmer for 40 minutes. It needs
longer because the bran layer is still on the grain.

Always rinse basmati rice until the water runs clear. Leftover rice must be cooled within an
hour and eaten within a day.
