# Oats

Oats are a whole grain. They are rich in soluble fibre, mainly beta-glucan, which is the
part that helps lower cholesterol and keeps you full for longer.

## Cooking

For creamy porridge, simmer rolled oats in milk for five minutes, stirring often. Use one part
oats to two parts liquid. Steel-cut oats take much longer, about twenty-five minutes.

For overnight oats, mix the oats with yoghurt and milk and leave the jar in the fridge for
eight hours. Nothing needs to be cooked.

## Storage

Keep oats in an airtight container in a cool, dry cupboard. Unopened rolled oats last about
twelve months before they start to taste stale.
