# Project Atlas

Atlas is the new customer onboarding platform.

## Goals

Reduce the time to onboard a new customer from 10 days to 4 days, and cut manual data entry by
half.

## Team

Priya is the project lead. Marco is the backend developer and Lena is the designer.

## Deadlines

The beta release is on 30 June. The public launch is planned for 15 September.

## Risks

The main risk is a delay from the payments vendor. Mitigation: agree a fallback provider by May.
